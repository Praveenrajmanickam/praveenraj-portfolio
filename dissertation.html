Heart Disease Risk Prediction

The problem

Heart disease is a leading cause of death, and lifestyle is something people can change. But do the same habits drive the risk in every country?

What I built

XGBoost and Random Forest models trained on lifestyle features from India, the USA, the UK and France, with feature importance used to rank the risk drivers.

The result

Smoking, obesity and physical inactivity came out as the major contributors to coronary heart disease risk.
